Why it's different

An instrument, not a library.

Built for doing, not reading. Six things separate it from every strategy deck and framework collection you've already been disappointed by.

01

Built for doing

Three questions and the router sends you to the one lens the problem needs — five in the app, not a library you have to read first. The app is the instrument played, not described.

02

Communication gets equal weight

Most strategy products stop at the decision. This one carries it to behaviour — one canonical artifact, kept true, that people can actually hold in their heads.

03

Frameworks stripped to working parts

Each lens reduced to its operative anatomy — and a self-test in the app that asks whether your diagnosis is a goal in disguise, or your pre-mortem an excuse. It is the one place the instrument says something uncomfortable about your own thinking.

04

Problem-first, not framework-first

Lenses, not answers. You start from the situation and reach for the right tool, instead of forcing every problem through a favourite model.

05

AI-native

The vault doubles as a context pack. Point your assistant at it and it challenges the work against the frameworks' own quality bars — not generic strategy soup.

06

A feedback spine that watches

Log the readings and the app reads them back: two breaches in a row and it proposes the signal is disagreeing, with the numbers behind it. Confirm, and the bet it watches is flagged too. It proposes — you decide. Nothing changes on its own.
